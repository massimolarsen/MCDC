# Distribution-mode SEU scoring

The CubeSat Geant4 output contains an ionizing-energy response, **not** a calibrated bit-upset rate. For each scored sensitive volume (SV), every Geant4 event contributes its primary and all descendant tracks to total energy deposition and NIEL. The ionizing score is `Edep_total - Edep_NIEL`. Event energies remain physical and unweighted; source weights enter only run sums and the per-SV histogram (`count`, `sumw`, `sumw2`). This assumes Geant4 does not split or roulette descendants within an event.

The HDF5 region file keeps the existing total-edep and dose datasets and adds `component_niel_mev`, `component_ionizing_mev`, depositing-species and primary/secondary ionizing sums, and `component_event_ionizing_*` histogram datasets. Histogram slots are zero, underflow below the first positive edge, regular log bins, and overflow. `electronics_cut_materials` and `electronics_cut_energy_mev` give the converted gamma, e-, e+, and proton thresholds for each material in the shared electronics region; the proton range cut is zero. The provisional CubeSat EM-only range cut is 0.001 mm. Species attribution can change with production cuts because an ungenerated secondary's energy is deposited locally by its parent track.

Geant4-only sampling errors use the Geant4 event as the independent observation, including events with zero SV deposition. `component_ionizing_sum_sq_mev2` and `component_species_ionizing_sum_sq_mev2` store sums of `(event_weight * event_Eion)^2`; `component_species_positive_events` counts events with positive ionizing deposition from each depositing species (not particles or steps). With `S` the existing weighted energy sum, `Q` its event-level squared sum, and `N=events_run`, the estimated standard error of `S` is `sqrt(N/(N-1) * max(0, Q - S*S/N))`. The histogram `sumw` and `sumw2` similarly give a Geant4-only error for threshold exceedances at bin edges. A zero species count has no meaningful relative error: for equal-weight independent source events its one-sided 95% occurrence upper bound is `1 - 0.05**(1/N)` (approximately `3/N`). These errors condition on the MCDC source distribution and do not cover uncertainty in that source, physics models, production cuts, or device response. Older HDF5 files lack the new species moments/counts and need Geant4 replay to obtain them.

Set `record_seu_events=True` to save `seu_diagnostics` tables for events whose maximum SV ionizing energy reaches `diagnostic_min_Eion_mev` (default 0.001 MeV). This selection never affects aggregate scores. `event_sv` links by `run_id`, `event_id`, and `sv_id`; `sv_entry` includes each boundary entry/re-entry and handoff primaries starting inside an SV, with track vertex/origin data. Tracks born inside an SV are not counted as entering it. `nuclear_birth` stores individual neutron, proton, alpha, d/t, and ion/recoil births anywhere in the modeled event. `em_secondary_summary` stores electron, positron, and gamma birth counts and creation-energy sums per selected event. `run_id` and `event_id` refer to the Geant4 region run, not MCDC history IDs.

Plot one region overview with `python diag/plot_seu.py path/to/region_geant4.h5 --output seu_summary.png`. It compares ionizing/NIEL deposition, depositing species, primary/secondary tracks, and the source-weight fraction of events depositing at least 1 keV in each SV. The overview adds one-standard-error bars for ionizing energy and threshold exceedance, plus a species panel showing positive-event counts and relative standard errors. The normalized species and ancestry fractions are descriptive, not confidence intervals for those ratios. Individual event-energy plots are opt-in: add `--sv sensitive_volume_name --output seu_sv.png`. Their selected-event panel is omitted when detailed records were not enabled. Device-sensitive geometry, charge collection, a calibrated critical charge, cut convergence, and physics-list benchmarking are separate work before interpreting these scores as SEU rates.
