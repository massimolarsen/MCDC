The projection study in \texttt{examples/rmc/basis\_study} estimates residual
norms of ideal approximations before implementing each trial space in RMC.
It is a screening calculation, not evidence of measured figure of merit.
For source sampling proportional to $|r|$, the squared source weight is
$\|r\|_1^2$. A transported response also has a conditional second moment and a
history cost depending on the birth state. Neither cancels automatically in a
comparison with SMC. Unknown count is a cost proxy, not elapsed time.

\paragraph{Projection quadrature.}
With spherical harmonics stored in $B$, quadrature weights $W$ must resolve
$B^TWB=I$ before using $B^TW$ as the projection. The former four-point product
panels had a maximum P7 Gram defect of $0.076586$. Eight-point panels reduce it
to about $9\times10^{-11}$; the constructor now rejects underresolved harmonic
projection. Existing saved results use the old quadrature and remain historical
until regenerated. A Gram check verifies the trial projection, not the full
scattering operator or transport accuracy.

\paragraph{Residual norms.}
The volume and face calculations use fine-cell averages before taking absolute
values. Jensen's inequality gives $|\langle r\rangle|\le\langle|r|\rangle$;
these are potentially underestimated norms, requiring independent refinement
or integration inside the fine cells. Fine-group transport is not exact
continuous-energy transport. In particular the fast analogue omits anisotropic
elastic laws, inelastic emission and fission production.

\paragraph{Characteristic trial verification.}
For an exact reference $\psi$, $\widetilde\psi=T^{-1}(q+\Pi S\psi)$ has residual
$(I-ST^{-1})(I-\Pi)S\psi$. The reference S\_N solve uses nonlinear negative-flux
fixup, while the signed streaming inverse cannot use that fixup. The corrected
study therefore constructs $\widetilde\psi$ explicitly and evaluates
$S\widetilde\psi-\Pi S\psi_{\rm ref}$. It does not assume the two discrete
operators agree. Source iteration checks all retained harmonic moments, and
signed streaming convergence checks the full iterate rather than a scalar norm
that may conceal an unconverged distribution. Failed convergence is reported.

\paragraph{Evidence required for an algorithmic claim.}
Verify projection reproduction, reference mesh/energy/angular convergence, direct
residual consistency, and numerical norm convergence. Then measure independent
transport estimates and all setup/evaluation costs on a prespecified matrix.
The characteristic-source prototype in
\texttt{cartesian\_3d/streaming\_source.tex} follows this second stage and includes
its randomized baseline and inner errors. Its one-group results do not validate
continuous-energy, reflective or multiplying extensions.
